### New findings

**Security and the record**

- **N1 (medium): who signed is not frozen.** `ReportSignature` covers the signer's id; the signature check (`ReportSignature.kt:144, 164`) and the PDF (`PdfExportService.kt:263-266`, and the site manager at 188-191) read the name and the ČKAIT number as they are now. After an edit of the user every earlier signed entry shows the new values, and for an account that got its number at creation nothing in the chain recorded the number (`user.create`, see S5 above; since A2 the `user.*` rows record it as it was at every change, but the PDF and the check still read the current one). **Fixed by format 3 (C2):** the name and the number are stored in the statement that signs and covered by the signature; the `report.lock` row of the audit chain holds them too. What format 3 does not cover (the author, the uploader of a photo, the people named under acknowledgements, addenda and entries of other parties) is stored when each record is made and printed as stored: **done by B9**.
- **N2 (low to medium): an administrator can give themselves a ČKAIT number** (`UserService.kt` blocks only a change of the own role, lines 236-238, and the removal of the own administrator flag, lines 223-225), join any project as BOSS (D1) and sign. Everything is audited, but the qualification becomes self-certified. **Narrowed by A1, not closed:** an administrator cannot change their own number, but can still qualify themselves in a second, audited step (a second administrator they mint, a password reset, a manager with an invented number); see `PROJECT.md`, Follow-ups.
- **N4 (low): uploads are buffered before any limit.** `PhotoRoutes.kt:63-87` reads up to 5 MiB into the heap before the access check and before the two-image semaphore; about 40 parallel uploads from any signed-in account, an INVESTOR's included, exhaust the heap, and the server restarts (an estimate from the 40 % heap of a 1 GB machine, not measured). **Fixed (A5):** bodies in flight are limited before the first byte is read.
- **N5 (low): managers who are gone.** `anotherManagerExists` (`ProjectMemberService.kt:75-81`) counts deactivated and deleted users, and a site manager can be deactivated (`UserService.kt:275-324`).
- **N5 (low): managers who are gone.** `anotherManagerExists` (`ProjectMemberService.kt:75-81`) counts deactivated and deleted users, and a site manager can be deactivated (`UserService.kt:275-324`). **Fixed by A1:** the count is of active users only; deactivating a site manager stays possible (the emergency switch) and warns, deleting one is refused.
- **N6 (low): text limits** are missing on `displayName`, `ckaitNumber` and the fields of authorised persons (`UserService.kt`, `AuthorizedPersonService.kt:63-75`).

**The law and the application** (details in `PROJECT.md`, "What an entry says" and "Entry dates and late entries")

- **L1: the decree is repealed from 1 January 2027** (sněmovní tisk 67, signed on 17 September 2026), with a transition until the nařízení vlády takes effect, at the latest until 31 December 2027.
- **L2: the late-entry rule is not the one of příloha 12 C.2** (the next day on which work is done; the app checks only when an entry is created).
- **L3: who writes.** A WORKER can add addenda in their own name, and workers are not among the persons of § 166 odst. 2. Entries of authorities, the BOZP coordinator or a surveyor are typed by the BOSS, which may not meet "jednoznačnost zápisu" (C.3).
- **L4: qualification.** Signing requires a ČKAIT number, which leaves out an architect of ČKA as stavbyvedoucí of a simple building and the stavební dozor of a self-help build. The stamp of part A is a one-off proof for every § 155 person, and the app records it for nobody.
- **L5: access.** Authorised persons without an account (the building authority at an inspection, C.1.b) cannot read the diary; the builder cannot name the persons who get the 10-year access of § 166 odst. 3; no kolaudace date is stored to count the 10 years from.
- **L6: the jednoduchý záznam o stavbě**, the second part of příloha 12, is not supported.

**The web application and the PDF** (seen in the running application)

- **W1: drafts are lost or overwritten.** There is no `beforeunload`; `BrowserRouter` (`main.tsx:26`) cannot block navigation inside the app; logout, and the "Odhlásit" button of the session dialog, throw a draft away (signing in again from that dialog keeps it); nothing is kept on the device, so a phone that kills the tab while the camera is open loses the entry. The form can be typed into before an existing entry has loaded (`DailyReport.tsx:548`), and a double tap sends the first save twice (`handleCreateReport` has no guard), the second one ending in a false "somebody created it meanwhile" conflict. _Fixed by B1 (loading, double saves, `beforeunload`) and B2 (navigation, drafts)._
- **W2: phones.** The header (`AppLayout.tsx:33-66`) and the project tabs (`ProjectDetail.tsx:386`) do not wrap: at 375 px a page inside the layout was 571 px wide with the E2E data, so the browser shrinks it by about a third, and the logout button is off the screen. _Fixed by B3._
- **W3: a signed entry is a disabled form.** Example text such as "např. Zedník" reads like content, the file input and "+ Přidat profesi" stay on the screen, the signer is shown only after "Ověřit podpis" (`DailyReport.tsx:778`), and INSPECTOR and INVESTOR get the same form (saving ends in `403`). _Fixed by B4._
- **W4: the PDF leaves out signed content.** The control-day flag and the construction object are covered by the signature (`ReportSignature.kt:107-108`) but not printed (`PdfExportService.kt:84-124`); a draft PDF says that it is one only in its title and status row, with no watermark. **Fixed by B8.**
- **W5: lists say little.** The entry list shows no number, state or late flag, although the API sends them (the author's name is not in the DTO); the project list shows nothing about today's entry, and nothing at all when it is empty; dates are ISO everywhere; an empty grey box shows in the project header (`ProjectDetail.tsx:369-382`). _Fixed by B4 (the box of the header was made conditional by B3 and is now pinned by a spec)._
- **W6: contrast and controls.** Input borders `gray-300` (about 1.5:1), white on `emerald-600` (about 3.6:1), `red-500` error text; a bare `border` in Tailwind 4 is `currentColor`, so modal inputs are almost black; labels not tied to their fields (`DailyReport.tsx:580, 746`); custom dropdowns that do not work from the keyboard (`ProjectDetail.tsx:880-944`, `NewProject.tsx:80-100`); 14 px inputs, on which iOS zooms; placeholders instead of labels on the login form. _Contrast, the default border colour, 16 px fields and 44 px buttons and fields are fixed by B3 for a finger (a coarse pointer, at any width, upright or sideways; a mouse on a wide screen keeps 36 to 40 px), also on the entry page through a rule in `index.css`; the labels of the entry form are for B5 and B7; the custom dropdowns are native selects since B6._
- **W7: two E2E assertions can never fail** (`legislative.spec.ts:192-193`).

## Bottom line

- **The architecture makes sense.** Ktor, jOOQ, Flyway and PostgreSQL 18 on one Fly machine, with the SPA served from the same origin and no Redis or queues, is a good fit for a single-company legal record. The audit hash chain is designed correctly: an advisory lock serializes writes, the audit row and the business change share one transaction, and the hash format matches the old TypeScript verifier.
- **The implementation is not safe for real diary data yet.** There are 4 critical and 10 high-severity findings:
  - the production image can't start;
  - photos can be downloaded without logging in;
  - a signed report can be re-signed;
  - sessions can't be revoked for 7 days;
  - any app admin can write and sign in every project;
  - PDF export allows code injection;
  - audit rows record nothing about what changed.
- **The root cause is structural.** There is no single write path. Permission, lock and audit checks are spread over the routes, service code that runs _before_ the transaction, and service code inside it. Most services skip the real membership check by passing a hard-coded `Resource(isMember = true)`. The plan below fixes that structure first, so these bug classes can't come back.
- **The legal basis in the docs is out of date.** New diaries fall under **zákon 283/2021 Sb. § 166** and **vyhláška 131/2024 Sb., § 10 + příloha č. 12**, in force since 1 July 2024. The README, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `ProjectService.kt:27` still cite § 157 of the old act and vyhláška 499/2006, příloha 16.

### What's solid

- The stack choice and the single-node deployment model.
- The audit hash chain:
  - its canonical JSON genuinely matches the legacy verifier for current rows;
  - `pg_advisory_xact_lock(42)` serializes appends, so id order equals commit order;
  - each audit row commits or rolls back together with its business change;
  - the UPDATE/DELETE triggers exist.
- No SQL injection surface (the only raw SQL is a constant), and the audit `limit` parameter is clamped.
- Uploads are re-encoded to a fresh JPEG: EXIF stripped, UUID file names, a magic-byte allow-list, SVG rejected.
- Login and sessions basics:
  - the JWT sits in an HttpOnly SameSite=Lax cookie with the algorithm and issuer pinned;
  - production refuses to start without `JWT_SECRET`;
  - passwords use Argon2id, and temporary passwords come from `SecureRandom`.
- Tests run the real migrations on real PG18. CI uses least-privilege permissions and per-job timeouts.

## Legal basis (to confirm with a lawyer)

These two points are confirmed against the primary text on zakonyprolidi.cz:

- § 166 of zákon 283/2021 Sb. is "Stavební deník".
- Vyhláška 131/2024 Sb. "o dokumentaci staveb" is in force since 1 July 2024. Its § 10 ("Stavební deník a jednoduchý záznam o stavbě") refers to **příloha č. 12** for the diary's content and how it is kept.

The rest comes from the critic's reading of the full texts and is **not yet confirmed**:

- Who may make entries: a wider circle than the app models, including TDS, autorský dozor, koordinátor BOZP and supervisory authorities.
- Handover to the stavebník in a **machine-readable form**, or **10 years** of access after kolaudace.
- Příloha 12 requires items the schema lacks:
  - names of the people working on site (not just counts per trade);
  - dust-control and accessibility measures;
  - permit date and reference number, subcontractors;
  - entries made the same or the next working day;
  - pages numbered without gaps.
- An entry made by a public authority would need a **qualified electronic signature** (zákon 297/2016 Sb. § 5). For private parties, OZ § 562 favours records that are made systematically and protected against change, which the hash chain plus database triggers provides.
- Transitional cases under § 330 (proceedings started under the old act) may still follow the old decree.
